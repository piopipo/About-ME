MY FAVORITE PAINTING

A Painting I Love

Claude Monet — Water Lilies

CLAUDE MONET • WATER LILIES • 1916
A little piece of Monet's world

I like art that feels alive.

One of my favorite paintings is Water Lilies by Claude Monet. I love how the painting doesn't feel completely still. The colors, reflections, water, and flowers almost feel like they're moving.

That's one of the things I love about painting: it doesn't always have to explain something. Sometimes it can simply make you feel something.
